# Skill: Negotiation Rules

Use this skill when implementing, evaluating, or refactoring the conflict resolution engine between the Health & Biosecurity Agent and Energy Optimization Agent.

## Core Mandate
- Health alerts (Ammonia $\ge 20\text{ ppm}$, $CO_2 \ge 3000\text{ ppm}$, Thermal stress) **ALWAYS** take absolute priority over energy cost curtailment.
- Cost-saving ventilation throttles during peak electrical tariffs must be safely executed only when welfare indices are strictly within nominal green bounds.
- All overrides must log deterministic audit logs with rule identifiers (`RULE_H01_AMMONIA_EMERGENCY`, `RULE_H02_CO2_OVERLIMIT`, `RULE_H03_THERMAL_SAFETY`).
